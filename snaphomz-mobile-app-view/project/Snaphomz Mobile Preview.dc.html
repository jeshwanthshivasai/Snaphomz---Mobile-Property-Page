rebuilt for a phone

The desktop page runs about 11,600px tall with a right-hand chat panel and an ad rail. Neither survives a 390px screen. This is the same information re-sequenced around one question a buyer asks first — is this worth a Saturday? — with everything else moved behind eight full-screen sheets.

What I assumed
Overview holds hero, signals and highlights; the eight tabs open as sheets.
Broadsheet sets the type and spacing; Snaphomz keeps its cards and its blue (#0018A8, sampled off your screenshot).
Copy is advisor-voiced, the way the desktop AI writeups already read.
Every estimate carries a source line. Nothing states a modelled number as a fact.
Signed out. Save prompts for an account; the tap is not lost.
Photos, map and figures are lifted from your screenshots. Rooms without a real photo show a labelled plate.
Three decisions worth arguing with
A verdict before the data. The first thing under the price is a two-line answer, not a card grid. Signals become the evidence for it.
Colour carries direction, not sentiment. Blue marks what favours the buyer, magenta what works against them. No green/red — it reads at a glance and survives colour blindness.
Failure is designed, not caught. Stale price, pending sale, broken calculator input and an abandoned form each have a written state. Trigger them on the right.
